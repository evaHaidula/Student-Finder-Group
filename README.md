# StudyFinder

StudyFinder is a student-focused platform designed to make it easier to find study resources, connect with other students, and discover study groups based on shared courses, subjects, or interests.
Features

 Search for study groups and resources
Connect with students taking similar courses
Organise and discover useful study materials
Create or join study groups
 Share information and collaborate with other students
 Designed with students and academic collaboration in mind

#Project Goals

StudyFinder aims to:

* Make academic collaboration more accessible
* Help students find relevant study communities
* Encourage peer-to-peer learning
* Provide a central place for discovering study resources
* Develop practical software development and collaboration skills
